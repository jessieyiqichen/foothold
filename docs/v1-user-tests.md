# v1 user tests (due 2026-11-17)

The brief: give each person the core task, watch without helping, write down what they did and said. Only what you saw goes here; no tidying, no guessing what they meant.

**The core task, said the same way to everyone:** "Here is a link. Use it to find startups you might want to reach out to, the way you would on your own. Think out loud." Then say nothing until they stop or ask to stop.

**Link:** https://foothold.vercel.app (each person signs up with their own email).

## Before each session

- Ask if you may take notes. Do not record anything they have not agreed to.
- They use their own resume. If they would rather not, they can paste a short made-up one; write down which.
- Note the device and browser.

## What to look for while watching

- Where they hesitate for more than a few seconds, and what they click next.
- Whether they read "Why it fits you" and "Who to contact", or skip straight to the role.
- What they do on a card with no contact found.
- Whether anyone uses Skip, Save and "I reached out" as intended, or opens the role link and leaves.
- Anything they say about the tone, the look, or trusting the information.
